## Worth re-measuring on your own engine

1. **Plastic runner length** along its axis with a string — the most influential parameter (≈ −95 rpm of peak per +10 mm).
2. **Chamber volume** with water in two steps: with the runners plugged and without.
3. **Lower manifold length** (injector module), flange to flange.
4. Length and diameter of the **throttle-to-filter pipe** — for the plenum resonance.
